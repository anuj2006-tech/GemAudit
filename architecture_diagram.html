🏛️ SIH 2026 Presentation Architecture

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE: GeM AUDIT

Detailed project workflow from PDF ingestion to multi-portal verification & officer decision

Step 01: Intake
Tender & Document Queue

Officer picks a live GeM tender. Uploaded bidder PDFs (CA Turnover Certificate, Udyam URN, GST returns, OEM MAF) enter the verification queue.

Step 02: AI Forensics
Gemini 2.5 Flash OCR

Extracts declared financial figures, registration IDs, and dates. Scans for font tampering, altered turnover numbers, or forged certificates.

Step 03: Verification
10+ Statutory Registries

FastAPI engine cross-references claims against GSTN (actual GSTR filings), MCA21 (Director DIN), Udyam (MSME), and CPPP (Blacklist).

Step 04: Action & Audit
Officer HUD & SHA-256 Seal

Officer reviews highlighted red flags and clicks Qualify, Clarify, or Disqualify. Cryptographic audit trail logged for CVC vigilance defense.
